\section{Related Work}
\textbf{Insect inspired navigation.} The ant toolkit comprises a skylight compass, a path integrator and view memory \cite{wehner03, muller07}. Sahabot \cite{lambrinos} and AntBot \cite{antbot} navigated with polarisation compasses, and recent work pairs odometry with learned views \cite{gattaux, firlefyn}; a review finds no study yet showing insect inspired navigation working robustly \cite{decroon}.

\textbf{Skylight polarisation as a compass.} Single scattering Rayleigh theory gives the e vector normal to the scattering plane \cite{suhai}; measured patterns deviate under aerosol and cloud \cite{horvath, pomozi, pust}, neutral points move with turbidity \cite{deleon25}, and vector radiative transfer with aerosol profiles reproduces measured skies \cite{zeng08}. Lower DoP means larger angle error \cite{ma15}; near the zenith the solar azimuth is undefined and yaw is lost \cite{liang20b, hu22}; tilt adds errors that grow with tilt and solar altitude \cite{liang}, and vision based tilt compensation has been proposed \cite{yuan25}. Multi directional sensors combined with a sun sensor target cloudy skies \cite{wang24}, and a fused pattern compass was recently tested in dust \cite{su26}. Engineered sensors report sub degree to a few degrees of heading error \cite{wang19, potter, hu22}, and a single camera compass already estimates its own covariance \cite{sturzl17}. Open full sky polarisation image sets now exist \cite{poughon24}.

\textbf{Heading in visual inertial estimation and its weighting.} Visual inertial filters \cite{mourikis, openvins, vinsmono, orbslam3} are overconfident in yaw without observability aware design \cite{hesch, huang_fej, limourikis}. Polarisation compasses have been fused with visual inertial filters on micro aerial vehicles \cite{steidle} and road vehicles \cite{chu23}. For weighting, covariance matching \cite{zhang20}, chi square based inflation \cite{amohead}, DoP thresholds \cite{zhang22}, variational Bayes and innovation saturation \cite{zhao, sun25} have been used, mostly without a camera. Planetary analogue datasets (MADMAX \cite{madmax}, S3LI \cite{giubilato26}) expose visual odometry to sand and glare.

\textbf{Gap.} We found no work, among those we opened, that couples a radiative transfer sky with dust to the camera degradation, weights a compass by the measured DoP inside visual odometry, and confirms the ranking on unseen environments under a frozen protocol. Each part has precedent, and we cite it; the combination and the confirmation are what this study adds.
