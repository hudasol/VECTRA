\section{Discussion}
\textbf{What survived the stronger tests.} The central Study~2 finding holds on unseen environments, with a sky that is no longer matched to the filter: a weighted sky compass cuts heading error by a factor of about \sfactorHone{} once the cameras degrade, and an ungated one is harmful. Bias makes the ungated compass harmful even with clear images, which Study~1 had already suggested. The choice among weighting schemes matters much less than having one: DoP weighting, covariance matching, the DoP floored covariance matching that Study~2 found by exploration, Huber, two mode and learned weighting all land within a few tenths of a degree, and their order changes between splits. Any of these weighting schemes is acceptable and none is established as best; B4 has the practical advantage of never rejecting a sample outright, but on the confirmation split it was not better than the others.

\textbf{The blind window is geometry, and its length is uncertain.} The window exists because a polarisation pattern becomes rotationally symmetric when the sun is at the zenith; at that instant no sky geometry gives heading, but a ring of off zenith points shrinks the operational window from one to two hours to minutes or, under the measured anchored sky, to none in Abu Dhabi, where the sun passes about a degree from the zenith. Its length depends on the near zenith DoP, where our radiative transfer model and the measured clear sky disagree by about 15 percent: the bracket for Abu Dhabi on 21 June is \sWinAnch{} to \sWinRT{} minutes at the mean June aerosol and \sWinMin{} to \sWinMax{} over the aerosol range. A measurement of zenith DoP with the sun within 10 degrees of the zenith, which neither data set we used contains, would close this bracket.

\textbf{Tilt is the practical limit near noon.} When the sun is high, a 2 degree tilt turns into a heading error of several degrees, larger than the photon noise of a sharp sensor. A compass whose noise model ignores tilt is gated out exactly when its geometry should help. Tilt compensation from the IMU attitude \cite{yuan25} and a tilt aware noise model are therefore not refinements but requirements for a ring sensor.

\textbf{The filter is still overconfident, and the cause is open.} Neither an oracle heading nor an oracle translation restores consistency; the visual forward speed, biased by 2 to 4 percent with a spread about four times the robust noise level, is a candidate cause. A scale bias state with a long time constant, or a visual noise level estimated from the spread rather than the median absolute deviation, is the next fix; the short correlation state we tested did not help.

\textbf{Ant style navigation is competitive when vision fails.} SIMULATED wheel odometry with assumed slip and a sky compass beat severely degraded stereo vision without a compass on drift (not with clear images), and in the SIMULATED homing study a tilt aware sky compass kept the error near 2 percent of a 3 km path on a March morning but reached 33 percent across the June noon window. This supports the ant inspired architecture as a fallback when cameras fail, with the noon window as its known gap.

\subsection*{Limitations}
Scenes are RENDERED and the TartanGround ground poses are level, so the filter is planar and no SE(3) or external visual inertial baseline was run; the compass, wheel odometry, sun sensor and magnetometer are SIMULATED with assumed parameters; the only MEASURED inputs are one day of Marseille sky images and two published desert DoP values; the aerosol climatology is REANALYSIS. The aerosol phase matrix is simplified and the dust optical depth is capped at 10. The confirmation split has three environments and 15 trajectories of at most 200 s with three degradation seeds. The weighting schemes differ by less than the variation between scenarios, so their relative order is not established.

\section{Conclusion}
In a frozen confirmation on \sNconf{} unseen rendered trajectories, with a radiative transfer sky, dust tied to the camera degradation, a compass bias and an untuned noise model, a weighted sky compass lowered the heading RMSE of degraded stereo visual odometry from \sHoneB{} to \sHoneA{} degrees, an ungated compass was harmful, a ring of off zenith points improved on the zenith point only when the sensor was level, and wheel odometry with a sky compass drifted less than vision without one. The noon blind window of a zenith compass, bracketed at \sWinAnch{} to \sWinRT{} minutes in Abu Dhabi on 21 June, occurs in the model at all latitudes within about \sLatMax{} degrees of the equator; a ring of off zenith points shortens it to minutes or less, but no polarisation geometry gives heading at the instant the sun is at the zenith. The filters remain overconfident; neither an oracle heading nor an oracle translation repairs this. The next steps are a near zenith sky measurement, tilt compensation, a visual scale bias state, and a real rover with a polarisation sensor in the Gulf summer.
